##### Visual:
- well-defined musculature
- male in the nude
- anatomically accurate
	- naturalism
- perfect male body
- follows beauty standards
- shiny material
- lighting (enhances)
- supported
- contrapposto
	- "counter-poise"
	- movement dynamism
	- uneven weight distribution
	- soft-s shape
	- support required (tree trunk)
- life-like
- free-standing (zero relief, no background)
##### Contextual:
- perfect male body -> idealisation/idealized form
	- conflate beauty with morals and virtues
	- good intellectual capacities
	- ratios worked out by sculptors like Polykleitos
		- agency of sculptors
		- to arrive at a canon (kanon)
- who?
	- soldiers, athletes, gods
- where?
	- courtyards, temples,
##### Practice:
Opening Lines: The Doryphoros is a Classical-age Greek sculpture by Polykleitos, which depicts a fully-nude male figure supposedly holding a spear on his left shoulder. It is notable for its life-like qualities, emphasising a realistic and detailed human figure while also demonstrating a sense of dynamism through its use of the *contrapposto*.
