Theories on Origins of Life
- Spontaneous Generation
	- Debunked
- Primordial Soup Theory
	- Experiment proved that simple organic molecules can arise from inorganic material
- External Source of Organic Molecules
	- Organic Molecules from Space
		- 92 amino acids - 19 used by life on earth
		- All 5 bases used in DNA/RNA found
		- simple sugars and fatty acids were found
	- Panspermia
		- "Seeds everywhere"
		- Life is distributed throughout space via germs or spores, and this primitive life forms could survive the journey through outer space
		- So where did life originate?
- How did life form?
	- How did chemistry become biology?
	- How did self replication begin?
- How do we solve this mystery?
	- Top-down approach
		- What do we share in common with other organisms?
		- How do these compare with the genetic make-up of organisms that live in conditions similar to the early Earth?
		- Can we identify the "minimum" genes
	- Bottom-up approach
