Chosen SDG: Goal 13 - Climate Action

Current situation:
- 2024 is the hottest year on record
- We have now passed 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels
- CO2 emissions are still increasing (2025 Global Carbon Budget)
	- China: +0.4%
	- India: +1.4%
	- US: +1.9%
	- EU: +0.4%
	- Rest of the world: +1.1%
	- 38.1 billion tonnes of fossil CO2 emissions projected in 2025
-Importance of the matter
- Still a smaller issue compared to contemporary issue, but there is the looming and increasing threat when nothing is done
- Already experiencing effects of climate change, Philippines one of the most vulnerable
	- Sinking coastlines over time
- Probably not causing human extinction, but will still cause unheard of levels of disasters and sufferings
	- Droughts, floodings, public health failure---furthers societal instability
- Pessimism:
	- We have less than 4 years left to meet the deadline of the 2015 Paris Agreement
		- Climate Clock: https://climateclock.world/
	- 95% of countries missing the deadline https://www.carbonbrief.org/analysis-95-of-countries-miss-un-deadline-to-submit-2035-climate-pledges/
	- Current policies only reduce temperature increase down to 2.5-2.9°C within the next century
		- Current pledges and targets: 1.8-2.1°C
Current and future challenges
- Rise of AI
- Continuous population growth
	- More energy and resources are needed, higher carbon footprint
- Conflicting corporate interests w/ climate sustainability
	- Fossil fuel lobbying (e.g. US)
