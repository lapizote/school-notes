# Tondo Commuity Initiative (Arielito H. Nunez)
### Smokey Mountain
- The symbol of Urban Poverty in the 80s, and everything after.
- Landfill area in Tondo, 2 million tonnes of trash collected from all around Metro Manila
- Closed in 1995
- Housing built around Smokey Mountain area
- "We gave them housing. Problem solved...?"
### Aroma Housing in Tondo
- People who were displaced due to the house construction in Smokey Mountain were placed here
- Nothing happens in Aroma that USM would not know, and vice-versa
- Was only decided to last for 3 years before relocation to permanent housing
	- Actually took 10 years

### Why are people still going to the cities? Why do the urban poor still exist?
- People are still willing to fit in the cities for more opportunities.
- Why does poverty still exist? The poor families expand. The poverty is inherited.
- Vices exist. Drugs are common. The biggest business besides junk shops.
- Hopelessness/victim mindset---they are used to relying to the government and NGOs charity.
- No security of water.
- Electricity is double the price as even these are exploited by the capitalists.
- They do not have space inside their houses.
- The children are numerous and do not get education. They do not even have a birth certificate! In the eyes of bureaucracy, they do not even exist!
- Why are they so patient? Maybe it has to do with the restricted space.
- Around 9000+ families still live in temporary housing.
- People still cannot separate themselves from trash.
