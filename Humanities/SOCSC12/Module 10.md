### Country Hill Tamils and Lines Rooms
- migrants from India during the British Occupation
- Tamils employed in the tea plantations in Sri Lanka
- Sustained class, ethnic, and class discrimination
- "Caste differentiation and identification infuse Hill Country Tamils' life and labor interactions with their employers, members of their plantation resident communities, and outsiders."
- Desire to move forward beyond the plantation.
	- The lack of a new generation of tea pickers opens the possibility for a mechanized future in the plantation.
- Poor living conditions in line rooms.
	- Sri Lankan government promised change.
- Hill-Country Tamils manages to create livable spaces in an unlivable environment.
	- Home-based rituals such as vayacu catanku is a testament to this.
		- "Cultural life."
		- Gendered acts of obligation within structures of kinship that affect the trajectory of a family's economic security and standing.

### Informal Settlement
- Around 500,000 families live in informal settlements in Metro Manila
- Wave of informal settlements started during the Marcos Sr. administration w/ the development of Manila as the "center" of the Philippines, "the City of Man", and where the narratives of progress and development resides.
- Most of informal settlers are from migrant families.
- Decades of informal settlement creates a sense of "home" within Manila slums.
	- Relocation is an issue, people are already grounded within the city.
- Government policy implementation is weak, research and statistical data is almost non-existent.
- The more people hide themselves, the more that the status quo is protected
