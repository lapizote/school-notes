##### Pure vs Physical Dipole
In a pure dipole, we assume that $r >> \text{loop radius} \,(\vec{m}=I\vec{a})$, but we take $I \to \infty$, $a \to 0$
This is similar to a pure electric dipole.

Meanwhile, in physical dipole, deviations start to appear when we close up onto the pole.
