### Experimental observations
When current is passed through parallel wires:
- Wires repel with antiparallel current
- Wires attract with parallel current
- Force is not electrostatic in nature (as wires are charge neutral)
- Magnetic force is generated

Empirical rules are followed for magnetic field and magnetic force through the right-hand rule.

### Lorenz Force Law
Defined as
$$
\vec{F}_\text{mag} = Q(\vec{v} \times \vec{B})
$$
(Note that this is an axiom based on observation, there is no proof)

Combining $\vec{E}$ and $\vec{B}$:
$$
\vec{F} = Q[\vec{E}+(\vec{v} \times \vec{B})]
$$
The consequences of this is the following:
- Magnetic force acts as a centripetal force in circular charge motion
- Magnetic forces do no work, $\vec{B}$ will only deflect a particle's direction
