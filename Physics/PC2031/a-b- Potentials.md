### Separation of Variables
The general solution for separation of variables for $V(r,\theta) = R(r)\Theta(\theta)$ is
$$
V(r,\theta) = \sum_{l=0}^\infty \left( A_lr^l + \frac{B_l}{r^{l+1}} \right)P_l(\cos \theta)
$$
where $P_l()$ refers to the Legendre polynomials with generating function
$$
P_l(x) = \frac{1}{2^ll!}\left( \frac{d}{dx} \right)^l (x^2-1)^l
$$
