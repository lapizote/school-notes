Planck's Constant: $h = 6.626 \times 10^{-34} \,\text{Js}$
de Broglie Wavelength: $\displaystyle \lambda = \frac{h}{mv}$
Photoelectric effect: $\displaystyle E = hf = \frac{hc}{\lambda}$
- Work function: $\phi = hf_\text{min}$
Light wavelength: $\displaystyle \lambda = \frac{hc}{E}$
Campton Effect: $\displaystyle \lambda = \lambda_0 + \frac{h}{m_ec}(1-\cos \theta)$
Blackbody Effect
- Wien's Displacement Law: $\lambda_\text{max} T = 2.898 \times 10^{-3} \,\text{mK}$
- Wien's Exponential Law: $\displaystyle I(\lambda) \propto \frac{1}{\lambda^5}e^{-hc/(\lambda kt)}$ (for low $\lambda$)
- Rayleigh-Jeans Law: $\displaystyle I(\lambda) = \frac{2\pi ckT}{\lambda^4}$ (for long $\lambda$)
- Max Planck's Interpolation: $\displaystyle I(\lambda) = \frac{2\pi c^2}{\lambda^5(e^{hc/(\lambda kt)}-1)}$
- Area under curve: $I_\text{total} = \sigma T^4$ (where $\displaystyle \sigma = 5.6704 \times 10^{-8} \,\frac{\text{W}}{\text{m}^2\text{k}^4}$)
